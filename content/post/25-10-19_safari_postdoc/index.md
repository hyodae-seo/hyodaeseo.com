[Seo Lab](https://www.hyodaeseo.com) in the Department of Oceanography at the University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa invites applications for a postdoctoral researcher to join the [ONR-funded SAFARI team (Study of Air–Sea Fluxes and Atmospheric River Intensity)](https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-32/division-322/marine-meteorology-space/sea-air-flux). [The UH SAFRI project](https://www.hyodaeseo.com/projects/onr_safari/) will utilize a combination of in situ and satellite observations, along with high-resolution coupled model simulations, to investigate the link between spatial variability of SST–wind–wave–current interactions and the diabatic intensification or dissipation of atmospheric rivers through air-sea heat and momentum fluxes. 

The successful candidate is expected to bring expertise in surface waves and air–sea interaction modeling, satellite remote sensing (e.g., SWOT), or analysis of in situ observations of air–sea fluxes, turbulent boundary layer processes, and/or their parameterizations. 

Applicants must apply [online](https://hr.rcuh.com/psc/hcmprd_exapp/EMPLOYEE/HRMS/c/HRS_HRAM_FL.HRS_CG_SEARCH_FL.GBL?Page=HRS_APP_JBPST_FL&Action=U&FOCUS=Applicant&SiteId=3&JobOpeningId=225530&PostingSeq=1&).
