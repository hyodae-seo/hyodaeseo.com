    <p>We are excited to host Prof. Changhuyn You from Ehwa Womans University in Seoul, South Korea, as a visiting scholar to Seo Lab at UHM. Prof. Yoo is a world-leading expert in climate and atmospheric dynamics, inclusing teleconnection patterns, Madden–Julian Oscillation and Rossby waves, and Hadley and Quasi-Biennial Oscillations. At UHM, Prof. Yoo and I will collaborate on a project to quantify the role of air-sea interaction, air-sea fluxes, and eddy momentum flux in shaping extratropical precipitation. Find out more about Prof. Yoo <a href="https://www.hyodaeseo.com/author/changhyun-yoo">here</a>.</p>
