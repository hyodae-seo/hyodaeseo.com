title: New Project ONR SAFARI DRI
date: 2025-07-02
---

<p style="font-size: 0.9em;">We are excited to be part of the new Office of Naval Research (ONR) Departmental Research Initiative (DRI) titled <strong>“Study of Air-Sea Fluxes and Atmospheric River Intensity (SAFARI)”</strong>. The SAFARI DRI seeks to advance both the physical understanding and predictive capabilities related to how air-sea fluxes and their interactions with turbulent boundary layer processes influence atmospheric river (AR) intensity and downstream impacts. Our project focuses on evaluating and improving parameterizations of air-sea fluxes mediated by surface waves and anomalous ocean conditions throughout the lifecycle of ARs. We aim to enhance the representation of air-sea interaction within coupled models to improve the simulation and prediction of AR behavior. Learn more about our project <a href="https://www.hyodaeseo.com/projects/onr_safari/">here</a>.</p>
